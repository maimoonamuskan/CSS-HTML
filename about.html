About Tourism Pakistan

Promoting the beauty, culture, and adventure of Pakistan.

Who We Are

Tourism Pakistan is dedicated to showcasing the breathtaking beauty of Pakistan. From the majestic mountains of Gilgit-Baltistan to the lush valleys of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Azad Kashmir, Pakistan offers unforgettable travel experiences.

Our mission is to inspire travelers to explore Pakistan's natural landscapes, cultural heritage, historical landmarks, and adventure destinations.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable tourism and provide visitors with accurate information about Pakistan's top destinations, culture, traditions, and travel opportunities.

Our Vision

To make Pakistan one of the world's leading tourism destinations by highlighting its natural beauty, hospitality, and cultural diversity.
